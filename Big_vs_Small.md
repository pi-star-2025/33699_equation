## Big vs Small

3.14159265 with 9265 is a choice of small (92) vs big (65).

92 goes as fast as possible from 3.1415. In addition, it assumes when going fast it will flip to another side, with the 2.

6 is the number that most mirrors this flip (a *slide* or *pipe*). This allows the 5 to "fall in" to looking at the 92.

Hence, 92 is fast (small) and 65 is slow (big) coming after the 92, by observing the flip.

...

This is similar to 3.14. 

2 sides are going fast towards one another, and the 4 (the c^2 mirror) slows down the process. What comes first (3) is smaller and is more light. What comes after (4) is from "falling in" and is more mass.

92 is like 3; 65 is like 4, but with 92/65 the 1 (originally from 3.14) has a fast side and slow side, where the slow side is based on c and the fast side goes faster based on e (explaining why it jumps/flips to 92).

...

In a way, the 9265 is most doing what the 1 of 3.14 is doing.
